Welcome! We are the Developmental Intergroup Processes (DIP) Lab from the University of Exeter. We are a psychology research group interested in how children and adolescents navigate, think about, and make decisions in their social and moral worlds.<br/><br/>In partnership with schools, families and community organisations, we conduct exciting research on a wide range of topics - including moral thinking about animals and nature, social inclusion, prosocial behaviour, STEM representation, and more.<br/><br/>We invite you to explore our website, discover our latest projects, and get in touch if you would like to collaborate or get involved! <img src="/images/IMG_7047.png" width="600" style="margin-top: 20px;">
