Welcome! We are the Developmental Intergroup Processes (DIP) Lab from the University of Exeter. We are a psychology research group interested in how children and adolescents think about and make decisions in their social and moral world.<br/><br/>With our partner schools and organisations we collect data for new and exciting research studies covering topics as diverse as moral thinking about animals and nature, social inclusion, prosocial behaviour, STEM representation, and more.<br/><br/>If this sounds interesting and you’d like to find out more, please explore our website and get in touch.
