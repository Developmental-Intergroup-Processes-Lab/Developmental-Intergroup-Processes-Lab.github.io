--- title: "Partnerships" layout: archive permalink: /year-archive/ author_profile: true --- Work at a school or community organisation and interested in taking part in research?

The Developmental Intergroup Processes Lab (DIP Lab) Partnership is a formal partnership between our research group and schools in the UK (and around the world!)

We understand that schools are very busy and want to make sure that this partnership really is a two-way street, so in return for your help we can provide:
  • Accessible reports on our findings
  • School assemblies tailored to your needs (in the past, for example, we have spoken about studying at university, inclusive classrooms, STEM representation)
  • Opportunities for older students to gain experience as 'research assistants for the day'
